## step 2: ServiceAccount that does not automount a token, and auth can-i

$ cat sa.yaml
apiVersion: v1
kind: ServiceAccount
metadata:
  name: catalog-sa
automountServiceAccountToken: false
[exit 0]

$ kubectl apply -f sa.yaml -n pap-a-cheat

[exit 0]

$ kubectl auth can-i get configmap/catalog-config -n pap-a-cheat --as=system:serviceaccount:pap-a-cheat:catalog-sa

[exit 1]
